reprokit

Reporter-side evidence tool for open-source bug reports. Built by a student in Bihar, India.

ReproKit does not reproduce bugs, execute code, or provide root-cause analysis. It produces the evidence a maintainer needs to attempt a reproduction, and says so plainly when the evidence is not there yet. Paste only public information — anything that looks like a credential is redacted server-side before storage.
